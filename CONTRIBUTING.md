# Contributing to India Simulator

## Branches
Use focused branches such as:
- agent/world
- agent/npc
- agent/gameplay
- agent/vehicles
- agent/missions
- agent/ui
- agent/economy
- agent/qa

Do not force-push or rewrite shared history unless explicitly authorized.

## Commits
Use focused conventional-style messages:
- feat: ...
- fix: ...
- refactor: ...
- test: ...
- chore: ...
