# INDIA SIMULATOR — STORY BIBLE

## Narrative direction
Build a character-driven story with layered personal, family, social, economic, and world-level conflicts.

## Narrative rules
- Characters must have goals, fears, contradictions, relationships, and consequences.
- Missions should change relationships or the world where appropriate.
- Avoid disposable NPCs for major characters; important characters remember meaningful player actions.
- The world and story must remain coherent across branches.

## Initial story work
The protagonist, district, principal cast, first mission, and long-term narrative arc are to be designed before large-scale mission implementation.
